Project Overview

Project Title: Official Surah Yasin & Tahlil Special Edition: Perak State Merdeka Celebration
Client: Jabatan Agama Islam Perak (JAIP) / Perak State Government
Category: Editorial & Government Publication Design

Commissioned by the Jabatan Agama Islam Perak (JAIP), this special edition Surah Yasin, Tahlil, and Doa booklet was custom-designed for the Perak State-Level National Day (Hari Kebangsaan) celebration. The design harmoniously bridges sacred Islamic literature with national identity.

The front cover features a dynamic, fluid integration of the Jalur Gemilang (Malaysian flag), wrapping elegantly around the central Arabic calligraphy. It symbolizes unity, faith, and patriotism, tailored specifically for the 2024/1446H state-level independence commemorations.
The back cover maintains a clean, minimalist corporate identity for the Perak State Government (Kerajaan Negeri Perak Darul Ridzuan). It features the official state emblem alongside institutional contact details, ensuring a highly professional and authoritative finish suitable for state dignitaries and the public.

Interior layout featuring a vibrant, custom ornamental border that frames the sacred text beautifully. The design incorporates a color-coded Tajweed guide at the footer, blending rich Islamic aesthetics with high functional readability.

A curated showcase of diverse Surah Yasin cover concepts, ranging from rich cultural heritages to modern state government editions. Each design explores a unique blend of custom Islamic geometry, fluid calligraphy arrangement, and tailored color palettes.

Project Overview

Project Title: Bespoke Al-Quran Cover: Contemporary Geometric Edition
Client: NZR SUPREME SDN.BHD
Category: Luxury Booksmithing & Editorial Design

A custom-commissioned Al-Quran cover design that reinterprets traditional Islamic art through a contemporary lens. Featuring a dynamic, multi-layered geometric vortex motif in varying shades of blue, this bespoke edition balances sacred geometry with modern, clean editorial aesthetics.

The front cover and spine utilize an intricate radial pattern that draws the eye toward the central Arabic typography. This structure is specifically optimized for premium print finishes, allowing for seamless integration of gold foil hot-stamping and textured paper materials.
A minimalist layout approach on a crisp white backdrop ensures the complex ornamental artwork remains the focal point. The design offers an elevated, premium feel suitable for exclusive gifting or high-end institutional publication.

The contemporary geometric framework and interior elements. Every line and vector in the radial motif is mathematically aligned to create an intricate sense of depth, tailored perfectly for luxury debossing and tactile print finishes.
